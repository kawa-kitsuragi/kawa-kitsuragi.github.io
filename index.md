# Things I've done

Eventually this site will have a Projects page with more details about each of the things in that resume, but in brief:

## Roguelike Celebration, 2017 - present

I've spoken at [Roguelike Celebration](https://roguelike.club) in 2017 (live fanfiction reading), 2018 ("A Trip to the Morgue: Considering Death in Roguelikes"), and 2019 ("A Conduct Conversation"). 

From 2020 onwards, I have been part of the organizing committee. I've touched almost every part of the process in the virtual conference, and now view a piece of my role as enabling new volunteers to take on more of the process and distributing institutional knowledge. 

## Strange Scaffold, 2021 - 2023

I was one of the first producers at [Strange Scaffold](https://www.strangescaffold.com/), helping a wide constellation of developers in every discipline ship a wide variety of small games in an efficient yet human-focused way. I was part of seven games across PC and console, primarily as a producer but also involved in studio management and a little bit of design and writing!

## Friday Night Funkin, 2023 - 2025

I was also the producer and administraion manager for [The Funkin Crew](https://funkin.me), taking the viral rhythm game *Friday Night Funkin'* from Newgrounds to the world. I managed the project as well as coordinating with logistics in merchandising, legal representation, and more.
